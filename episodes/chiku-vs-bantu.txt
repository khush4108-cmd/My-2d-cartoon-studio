Scene: jungle
Narrator: Deep in the jungle, Bantu the big bear is sleeping under a mango tree.
Bantu: Mmm... mangoes... (sleepy)
Narrator: Chiku the little monkey quietly steals Bantu's mango.
Chiku: Hehehe, too easy! (happy, sneak)
Narrator: Bantu wakes up and sees an empty hand.
Bantu: My mango... gone?! (surprised, jump)
Chiku: Finders keepers, big bear! (smug, dance)
Bantu: Come back here, you little monkey! (angry, run)
Narrator: Chiku jumps from branch to branch. Bantu crashes into a tree. CRASH!
Bantu: Owww... my head... (dizzy)
Chiku: Ha ha ha! Too slow, Bantu! (happy, dance)
Narrator: Bantu picks up a big pie from a picnic basket.
Bantu: Now I will get you! (determined, pounce)
Chiku: Not the pie! Not the pie! (scared, run)
Narrator: SPLAT! The pie lands on Bantu's own face.
Bantu: I missed? (surprised)
Chiku: Best throw ever! Ha ha! (excited, dance)
Scene: night
Narrator: At night, both sit by a warm fire and share the last mango.
Bantu: Friends taste better than mangoes. (happy)
Chiku: Almost! (smug)
Bantu: Goodnight, Chiku. (sleepy)
Chiku: Goodnight, Bantu! (excited, wave)
