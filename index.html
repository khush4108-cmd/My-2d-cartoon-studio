Type a story → press one button → watch the cartoon → save it as a video (MP4).

Try a ready story:
Cartoon kaise banti hai? (5 steps — yeh app yahi karta hai)
  1. Story / Script — pehle kahani likhte hain. Har line: Naam: dialogue (mood, action). Poori video ki neev yahi hai.
  2. Storyboard & Layout — kaunsi scene kahan hogi (jungle, park, room…), kaun left me khada hoga, camera kya karega.
  3. Character design + Rig — har character ka look fix (skin, baal, kapde). Uske body parts (sir, haath, pair) ko “rig” se hilaya jata hai — yahi characters.js + acting.js karte hain.
  4. Animation (poses) — har dialogue ke liye mood + action se pose banti hai, aur time ke saath woh move karti hai (walk cycle, squash-stretch, blink, lip-flap).
  5. Sound + Edit + Export — background music, comedy sound effects (CRASH! BOING!), cartoon awaaz, phir video file (MP4) me save.

Tom & Jerry wala “feel” 5 cheezon se aata hai: (1) squash & stretch — jump par body stretch, landing par dabna; (2) anticipation — action se pehle chhota sa peeche jhukna; (3) reaction shot — ek maar khata hai, doosra haste hue dikhta hai; (4) sound words — CRASH, BONK, BOING screen par; (5) timing — ek frame ka rukna = comedy.

Yahan tips: “Run!”, “jump”, “fell”, “cake”, “hammer”, “pie”, “dance” jaise shabd likho — app khud action, prop aur sound effect laga deta hai. Acting hint se control karo: (angry, pounce), (happy, dance), (scared, run). Naya scene: Scene: beach — jungle, park, room, night, beach, street, school.

Apna drawing add karna hai? js/characters.js me drawing badlo (ya naya character style banao), js/scenes.js me naya background — app phir bhi wahi video banayega.
