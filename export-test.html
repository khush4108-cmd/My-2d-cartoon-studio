3. Record the cartoon to a video file

Two honest limitations. 1. The spoken voices cannot be captured. speechSynthesis sends its audio straight to the system speaker and never enters the Web Audio graph, so no browser can put it in a recording. A downloaded file therefore contains the picture, the sound effects and the scene ambience — but not the dialogue. The measurements below are taken from the audio that actually went into your file, so you can see this for yourself rather than take it on trust. 2. On iPad, Safari records to .mp4, not .webm. The buttons above report which format your browser chose.
